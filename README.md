# Data-Analytics-Project
From Idea to Impact: 5 Steps to Build and Share Your Programming for Data Analytics Project

-Define the problem. Every good project starts with a question. Choose a topic that matters to you. Frame a clear problem statement and set your objectives.
-Find your data on Kaggle. Explore Kaggle to find a dataset that fits your question. Download it and check its quality and licence. It becomes your real-world case study.
-Code in Google Colab. Use Python to clean, explore and visualise the data. Build your graphs and models, then interpret what the results really mean.
-Publish on GitHub. Once the project is ready, store it in a well-organised repository. Include your notebook and a clear README, so anyone can understand and reproduce your work.
-Share on LinkedIn. Present your key insights with a visual and link to your GitHub repository. Your project then becomes part of your professional portfolio.
-From reflection to publication: a data science project is only complete when others can see it. As we say in French, « Le savoir-faire, il faut le faire savoir. » Know-how must be made known.
